# Gathering mark 01

The wall at `artifacts/gathering/wall.png` is an RGB PNG, 1254 × 1254 pixels, made from the supplied bare cave. One filled ochre and red-ochre Pepe sits near the open center with four small gathered stones. His broad mouth, large heavy-lidded eyes and two hands are visible; each hand has four fingers and one thumb. No letters, numbers, logo, frame, or other figure was painted.

I viewed the finished wall and compared decoded pixels with the cave: 38,446 pixels changed, all within `(495, 301)–(790, 560)`; every pixel outside the new mark is identical. The cave texture shows through worn pigment, and the edges are deliberately irregular. No visual requirement is known to be unmet.
